## Why

LAB-3202 (https://linear.app/protocollabs/issue/LAB-3202): the Home Top tab uses the same 5 editorial top stories as the digest.

## What Changes

- The Top pill and the "Top story" badge cover editorial ranks 1 to 5.
- The signed-out This week list leads with those ranks.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `home`: the Top pill and This week's editorial ranks.

## Impact

A story with editorial rank 4 or 5 appears on the Top pill and carries the "Top story" label. The old Top stories band still shows three cards.
